Resource FAQ

Are these resources a substitute for therapy? add

No. These articles and guides are meant to be educational tools to supplement your mental health journey. They are not a replacement for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Who writes these articles? add

All of our content is written or medically reviewed by licensed therapists and clinical psychologists on the STACKLY Wellness team.

Can I share these resources with someone else? add

Absolutely. If you think a guide or article could help a friend or family member, we encourage you to share it.
