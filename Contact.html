Contact & Booking FAQ

Do you accept insurance? add

We are out-of-network providers for most major insurance plans. We provide superbills that you can submit to your insurance company for reimbursement depending on your out-of-network benefits.

What are your session rates? add

Our standard rates range from $150 to $220 per 50-minute session, depending on the therapist's licensure and experience level. We also offer a limited number of sliding scale spots based on financial need.

What is your cancellation policy? add

We require a 24-hour notice for any cancellations or rescheduling. Sessions cancelled with less than 24 hours notice are subject to the full session fee.

Crisis Support

If you are experiencing a mental health emergency, please do not wait for an appointment. Immediate help is available.

Call or Text 988 (Crisis Lifeline)

Or proceed to the nearest emergency room.
