How Different Approaches Work

Not every approach works for every person. That's why our therapists often blend methods to suit your specific needs.

CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy)

Focuses on identifying and changing negative thought patterns to influence behavior and emotions.

Psychodynamic

Explores how past experiences and unconscious thoughts influence current behavior.

Mindfulness-Based

Uses meditation and mindfulness techniques to help you stay grounded in the present moment.

Choosing the Right Therapist

Research shows that the relationship between you and your therapist is the most important factor in the success of your therapy. It's perfectly okay to ask questions during your first session to ensure they are the right fit for you. If they aren't, we'll gladly help you find someone else on our team.
