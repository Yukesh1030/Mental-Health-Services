Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose a therapist? add

We provide a short matching questionnaire that considers your specific needs, preferences, and therapy goals to recommend specialists who would be a great fit for you.

What happens during the first session? add

The first session is largely about getting to know each other. Your therapist will ask about your background, what brought you to therapy, and your goals, while providing a safe space for you to ask questions.

Can I attend sessions online? add

Yes, all of our therapists offer secure, confidential video sessions. You can choose to do therapy entirely online or a mix of online and in-person.

How long is a typical session? add

Standard individual therapy sessions are 50 minutes long. Couples and family sessions may occasionally be scheduled for longer durations.

Can I change therapists? add

Absolutely. The therapeutic relationship is crucial. If you feel your current therapist isn't the right fit, we will help you transition to someone else on our team.

How do I know what type of support I need? add

You don't have to have it all figured out. Reaching out for an initial consultation is the first step, and our intake coordinators will help guide you toward the right care path.
